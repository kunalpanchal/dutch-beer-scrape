About us

The Pub: Where our craft comes to life

Step into our taproom ‘The Pub’ at Boterdiep 75 in Groningen, where our passion for bold, modern beers meets a vibrant atmosphere. With 16 rotating taps, enjoy our latest releases, small-batch exclusives and select guest beers from breweries we admire. Pair your drink with our kitchen’s offerings, all crafted to complement our brews.
